## What This Does

A central index of articles I have published on LinkedIn, covering data analytics, process
automation, AI, and adjacent technology and business topics. The index is the deliverable —
the articles themselves live on LinkedIn.

## Limitations

- **Links only, no article text.** Nothing is archived here. If LinkedIn changes a URL
  scheme or an article is removed, the link breaks and the content is not recoverable from
  this repository.
- **URLs carry LinkedIn tracking parameters.** Both links include a `trackingId` query
  string from the session where they were copied. They work, but they are not canonical.
- **Index is incomplete and not maintained on a schedule.** Only two articles are listed;
  it is updated by hand when remembered, so it will lag behind what is actually published.
- **No dates or topic tags.** Entries are unordered with no publication date, so there is no
  way to tell how current an article is without opening it.
